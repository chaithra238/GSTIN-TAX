What Is the GST Identification Number (GSTIN)?

GSTIN or Goods and Services Tax Identification Number is a unique 15-digit alpha-numeric PAN-based code allotted to every registered person under Goods and Service Tax (GST). This GST number is provided to the GST taxpayer along with the GST registration certificate, and the GST number also allows tracking of a GST-registered Taxpayer. GSTIN Structure/GST Number Structure

It has also been noticed that tax evaders have come up with new ways to evade taxes from people and ultimately fool consumers and the Government. They just put a fake alpha-numeric code against the GSTIN heading to misguide the consumers, which unarguably resembles a GSTIN. It is also vital to know how a GSTIN is structured to spot any malpractices.

Now let us understand what reveals a 15-character GSTIN or GST Number:

  • The first two digits stand for the unique state code in accordance with the Census of India, 2011. For instance, the state code of Punjab is 03, and that of Maharashtra is 27.
  • The next ten characters reflected are the PAN number of the taxpayer.
  • The 13th digit depicts the registration number of the taxpayer with the associated PAN number.
  • The 14th digit “Z” is by default for everyone – not representing anything currently.
  • The 15th digit is the Check digit –It can be an alphabet or a number.
