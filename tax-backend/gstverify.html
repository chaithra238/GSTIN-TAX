What This Tool Verifies

Registration Status

Confirms whether the GSTIN is Active, Cancelled, or Suspended — the most critical check before any transaction.

Legal & Trade Name

Shows the official legal name as filed with GST authorities, plus the trade name if the business operates under a different brand.

Taxpayer Type & Constitution

Reveals whether the entity is a Regular taxpayer, Composition dealer, SEZ unit, Government entity, or any other category.

Principal Business Address

The registered principal place of business — including building, street, locality, district, state, and PIN code.

Registration & Update Dates

Check when the GSTIN was first registered and the date of the most recent update to the GST record.

Nature of Business Activities

Lists the declared nature of business activities — useful for understanding the core business of a supplier or customer.
